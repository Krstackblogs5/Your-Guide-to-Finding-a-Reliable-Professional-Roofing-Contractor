Signs You Might Need a Roofing Contractor

If you’re wondering whether it’s time to call a roofing professional, here are some telltale signs to look out for:

Leaks or water stains on your ceilings or walls
Missing or damaged shingles after a storm
Sagging areas on your roofline
Visible mold, moss, or algae growth
Granules in gutters indicating shingle deterioration
A roof that’s over 15–20 years old, depending on the material
Even if you’re not dealing with an emergency, a roof inspection can help identify small issues before they become expensive repairs.

Tips for Maintaining Your Roof Long-Term

Once your new roof is in place, there are a few simple things you can do to keep it in good shape:

Schedule annual inspections. We recommend having your roof inspected at least once a year, especially before storm season.

Keep gutters clear. Clogged gutters can cause water to back up under your shingles and damage the roof deck.

Trim overhanging branches. Tree limbs can damage shingles or allow animals easy access to your roof.

Check attic ventilation. Poor airflow can cause heat buildup and moisture problems that weaken your roof.

Address issues promptly. If you notice a small leak or damage, don’t wait—call us. Minor fixes now can save you big money later.
We’re always happy to provide guidance on how to extend the life of your roof.
